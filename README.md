# India’s CPI Inflation Analysis (2014–2025)

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation rates from **2014 to 2025** using Python. The analysis explores annual and monthly inflation trends, year-to-year changes, monthly variability, COVID-19 period inflation, and relationships between monthly inflation rates.

The project demonstrates the use of Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to extract meaningful insights from real-world inflation data.

##  Objectives

* Analyze annual average inflation trends from 2014 to 2025.
* Identify the years with the highest and lowest annual inflation.
* Analyze average inflation across different months.
* Identify the highest- and lowest-inflation months for each year.
* Calculate year-to-year and annual percentage changes.
* Examine inflation during the COVID-19 period.
* Measure monthly inflation variability using standard deviation.
* Analyze relationships between monthly inflation rates using correlation.
* Visualize important trends and patterns.

##  Technologies Used

* **Python**
* **Pandas** — data cleaning, transformation, and analysis
* **Matplotlib** — data visualization
* **Seaborn** — correlation heatmap and statistical visualization
* **Google Colab** — development environment

##  Analysis Performed

### Annual Inflation Analysis

* Annual average inflation
* Highest and lowest annual inflation
* Year-to-year inflation changes
* Annual percentage changes
* Overall average inflation
* Annual inflation trend and comparison

### Monthly Inflation Analysis

* Average monthly inflation
* Highest and lowest average inflation months
* Highest-inflation month for each year
* Lowest-inflation month for each year
* Frequency of monthly inflation peaks and lows

### COVID-19 Period Analysis

Inflation during **2019–2021** was analyzed to examine changes before, during, and after the major COVID-19 disruption.

### Monthly Variability Analysis

Standard deviation was calculated for the twelve monthly inflation rates of each year to measure the degree of monthly fluctuation.

### Correlation Analysis

Relationships between monthly inflation rates were examined using:

* January–December scatter plot
* Monthly correlation matrix
* Monthly correlation heatmap

##  Key Findings

* **2014** recorded the highest annual average inflation during the study period.
* **2025** recorded the lowest annual average inflation.
* Annual inflation experienced considerable fluctuations between 2014 and 2025.
* The largest increase in annual average inflation occurred between **2019 and 2020**.
* **January** had the highest average monthly inflation, while **November** had the lowest.
* **2014** showed the highest monthly inflation variability, while **2015** showed the lowest.
* A notable increase in annual inflation was observed in **2020** during the COVID-19 period.
* **July and August** showed a very strong positive correlation of approximately **0.97**.
* **January and December** showed a weak negative correlation of approximately **−0.22**.
